Niceone Obsidian

Intelligence with governance built in.

Niceone Obsidian Intelligence System (OIS) is a project focused on governed AI workflows, automation, and social intelligence. Its design principle is simple: model output is a proposal—not permission or proof of success.

Responsible execution

OIS is designed around authorization, policy boundaries, evidence, validation, recovery, and controlled change. Specific integrations and features depend on the deployment and permissions configured by the operator.
